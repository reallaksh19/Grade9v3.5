# Reviewing a learner render

1. Confirm the render digest in the receipt and the review; a changed digest needs a new observation.
2. Read the author's design note, self-critique and step-back answers. Identify the inference each Core protects.
3. Attempt questions before opening hints and answers. Compare figure stages, options, source hints, captions and print modes against the learner's actual state. Look in DOM and accessibility text as well as on screen.
4. Independently solve numerical claims and cross-check the printed key, source card and verified result.
5. Describe at least three concrete strengths if found; do not invent strengths to fill the count. Group a repeated defect mechanism into one finding listing records. Each finding states severity, learner impact and an actionable suggestion.
6. Compare with `golden/INDEX.md` and anti-pattern cards when available. A determined learner bypassing an honest-self-learner commitment is outside the attempt threat model.
7. Keep earlier findings' statuses and your recommendation distinct. Neither recommendation nor an S1 is a publication gate; the Owner sees the evidence and chooses.
