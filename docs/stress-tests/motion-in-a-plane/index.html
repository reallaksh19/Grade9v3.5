Trajectory Geometry & Parabolic Kinematics
6. A person projects a bottle into a dustbin at the same height as he is 2m away at an angle of 45°. The velocity of projection is (in m/s):
  • (a) g
  • (b) \sqrt{g}
  • (c) 2g
  • (d) \sqrt{2g}
Official Key: (d) \sqrt{2g}
Independent Derivation: Range formula on horizontal ground is R = (u^2 * sin(2*theta)) / g. For theta = 45°, sin(90°) = 1, so R = u^2 / g. Given R = 2m, we have 2 = u^2 / g => u^2 = 2g => u = sqrt(2g). Option (d).

7. A ball of mass 'm' is thrown vertically upwards. Another ball of mass '2m' is thrown up making an angle 'theta' with the vertical. Both of them stay in air for the same time. Their maximum heights are in the ratio:
  • (a) 2:1
  • (b) 1:2
  • (c) 1:cos\theta
  • (d) 1:1
Official Key: (d) 1:1
Independent Derivation: Time of flight depends purely on vertical velocity component: T = 2*u_y / g. Since T1 = T2, u_1y = u_2y. Maximum height H = u_y^2 / (2g) also depends strictly on u_y. Hence H1 : H2 = u_1y^2 : u_2y^2 = 1:1, independent of mass or launch angle. Option (d).

8. From the top of a building 80m high, a ball is thrown horizontally which hits the ground at a distance. The line joining the top of the building to the point where it hits the ground makes an angle of 45° with the ground. Initial velocity of projection of the ball is (g=10 m/s^2):
  • (a) 10 m/s
  • (b) 15 m/s
  • (c) 20 m/s
  • (d) 30 m/s
Official Key: (c) 20 m/s
Independent Derivation: Line from top of building to impact point makes 45° with ground => tan(45°) = height / range => 1 = 80 / x => x = 80m. Time to fall height h = 80m is t = sqrt(2h/g) = sqrt(160/10) = 4s. Horizontal velocity u = x / t = 80 / 4 = 20 m/s. Option (c).

9. A stone is projected from the top of a tower with velocity 20 m/s making an angle of elevation of 30° with the horizontal. If the total time of flight is 5s and g=10 m/s^2, then:
  • (a) the height of the tower is 75m
  • (b) the maximum height of the stone from the ground is 80m
  • (c) both the above are true
  • (d) the height of the tower is 120m
Official Key: (c) both the above are true
Independent Derivation: u_y = 20 * sin(30°) = 10 m/s. Vertical displacement upon reaching ground: -h = u_y*t - 0.5*g*t^2 = 10(5) - 5(25) = 50 - 125 = -75m => h = 75m. Maximum height above tower: h_max = u_y^2 / (2g) = 100 / 20 = 5m. Height above ground = 75 + 5 = 80m. Thus both statements (a) and (b) are true. Option (c).

21. A particle of mass 1 kg is projected at an angle 45° to the horizontal with an initial velocity of 20 m/s. Change in momentum during its time of flight is:
  • (a) 10\sqrt{2} kg m/s
  • (b) 20\sqrt{2} kg m/s

  • (d) 40\sqrt{2} kg m/s

29. From the top of a tower of height 78.4m two stones are projected horizontally with 10 m/s and 20 m/s in opposite directions. On reaching the ground, their separation is:
  • (a) 60 m
  • (b) 100 m
  • (c) 120 m
  • (d) 200 m
Official Key: (c) 120 m
Independent Derivation: Both stones fall the same vertical height 78.4m under gravity: t = sqrt(2h/g) = sqrt(2 * 78.4 / 9.8) = sqrt(16) = 4s. Horizontal displacements in opposite directions: x1 = 10 * 4 = 40m, x2 = 20 * 4 = 80m. Total distance between them = x1 + x2 = 40 + 80 = 120m. Option (c).

5. A train is moving with a speed of 40 km/h due east. A bird is flying due north with a speed of 40 km/h. What is the velocity of the bird noted by a man in the train?
  • (a) 40\sqrt{2} km/h N-E
  • (b) 40\sqrt{2} km/h S-E
  • (c) 40\sqrt{2} km/h N-W
  • (d) 40\sqrt{2} km/h S-W
Official Key: (c) 40\sqrt{2} km/h N-W
Independent Derivation: Train velocity v_t = 40 i_hat km/h. Bird velocity v_b = 40 j_hat km/h. Relative velocity of bird with respect to train v_bt = v_b - v_t = -40 i_hat + 40 j_hat. Magnitude |v_bt| = sqrt(40^2 + 40^2) = 40*sqrt(2) km/h. Direction has negative east (west) and positive north => North-West (N-W). Option (c).

6. A swimmer can swim in still water at 4 km/h. To cross a river of width 1 km flowing at 2 km/h along the shortest possible path, in which direction should he swim?
  • (a) At an angle 120° to the water current
  • (b) At an angle 150° to the water current
  • (c) At an angle 90° to the water current
  • (d) None of these
Official Key: (a) At an angle 120° to the water current
Independent Derivation: Shortest path requires zero drift: swimmer must head upstream at angle theta with the normal such that v_s * sin(theta) = v_r => 4 * sin(theta) = 2 => sin(theta) = 1/2 => theta = 30° upstream from normal. Total angle with respect to downstream water current = 90° + 30° = 120°. Option (a).

7. A river flows with a speed greater than the maximum speed with which a person can swim in still water (v_r > v_s). He intends to cross the river by the shortest possible path. Which of the following is correct?
  • (a) He should start normal to the river bank
  • (b) He can reach the opposite point directly
  • (c) He should swim at sin(theta) = v_s/v_r upstream
  • (d) The man cannot reach the directly opposite point without drift
Official Key: (d) The man cannot reach the directly opposite point without drift
Independent Derivation: To reach the directly opposite point, zero net drift is required, meaning v_s * sin(theta) = v_r => sin(theta) = v_r / v_s. But v_r > v_s implies sin(theta) > 1, which is impossible for any physical heading angle. Therefore, drift cannot be eliminated and the swimmer cannot reach the directly opposite point. Option (d).

21. A projectile can have the same range R for two angles of projection. If t1 and t2 are the times of flight in the two cases, then:
  • (a) t1 * t2 \propto R
  • (b) t1 * t2 \propto R^2
  • (c) t1 * t2 \propto 1/R
  • (d) t1 * t2 \propto 1/R^2
Official Key: (a) t1 * t2 \propto R
Independent Derivation: Angles are theta and 90° - theta. t1 = 2u*sin(theta)/g, t2 = 2u*cos(theta)/g. Product t1 * t2 = (4u^2 * sin(theta)*cos(theta)) / g^2 = (2/g) * (u^2 * sin(2*theta) / g) = (2/g) * R. Thus t1 * t2 is directly proportional to R. Option (a).

47. The horizontal range and maximum height attained by a projectile are R and H, respectively. If a constant horizontal acceleration a_x = g/4 is imparted by wind, the new range is:
  • (a) R + H
  • (b) R + 2H
  • (c) R + 4H
  • (d) R + H/2
Official Key: (a) R + H
Independent Derivation: Flight time is governed strictly by vertical gravity: T = 2*u_y / g. Additional horizontal displacement due to wind acceleration: Delta_x = 0.5 * a_x * T^2 = 0.5 * (g/4) * (2*u_y / g)^2 = (g/8) * (4 * u_y^2 / g^2) = u_y^2 / (2g) = H. Therefore, new total range R_new = R + Delta_x = R + H. Option (a).
