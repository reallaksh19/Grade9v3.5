## 2. Mathematical Family Clusters

1. **Quadrant Sign Rules & Axis Identification**: Points \((x, y)\) categorized into Quadrants I \((+,+)\), II \((-,+)\), III \((-,-)\), IV \((+,-)\) or axes (\(x=0\) on y-axis, \(y=0\) on x-axis).
2. **Metric Projections & Axis Distances**: Perpendicular distance of \((x, y)\) from x-axis is \(|y|\), from y-axis is \(|x|\). Foot of perpendicular on y-axis is \((0, y)\).
3. **Geometric Figures in the Coordinate Plane**: Plotting vertices of rectangles and right triangles; computing enclosed areas via base and height.
4. **Coordinate Translation & Motion**: Steps required to navigate from origin to \((-a, -b)\).
5. **Lines & Incidence**: Number of lines passing through a single point (infinitely many).
