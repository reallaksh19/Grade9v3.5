🪜 The 5-Rung Pedagogical Scaffolding Ladder

Every authentic problem in Mole Concept & Stoichiometry is governed by this canonical 5-rung ladder. Each stage resolves specific student cognitive obstacles without prematurely revealing final answers.

Rung Stage Microtopic Focus & Core Concept Pedagogical Role & Learner Support Action
R0 / H0 · Foundation Mass-Mole Amount Conversion
Coordinate Frame & Visual Schema		 Convert measured mass into chemical amount n = m / M using standard atomic and molecular weights.
R1 / H1 · Connect (Laws) Stoichiometric Ratio & Limiting Reagent
Law & Constraint Binding		 Establish balanced equation molar ratios; identify limiting reactant via min(n_i / nu_i).
R2 / H2 · Transform Theoretical Yield & Concentration
Algebraic & Numerical Execution		 Calculate product masses, solution molarity M = n/V, molality m = n/m_solvent, and percentage yields.
R3 / H3 · Check (Verify) Conservation of Mass & Atom Balance
Dimensional & Extreme Limits		 Verify mass balance: sum of reactant masses equals sum of product masses; verify invariant total atom counts.
R5 · Mastery (Crux) Gas Eudiometry & Sequential Reactions
Competitive JEE / Olympiad Crux		 Solve multi-step chained reactions, gas combustion volume contraction in eudiometer tubes, and back-titration assays.
