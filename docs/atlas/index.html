Curriculum Atlas & Pedagogical Rungs

Comprehensive capability matrix, progressive hint ladders, and difficulty scaffolding across every subject and subtopic.

🪜 The 5-Rung Pedagogical Scaffolding Ladder

Every authentic question in the Question Bank is backed by an authored scaffolding ladder. When students encounter obstacles, they reveal only the precise rung needed to unblock their reasoning.

Rung Stage Pedagogical Role Learner Support Action
R0 / H0 · Foundation (Represent) Visual & Coordinate Model Establishes reference frame, sign conventions, state vectors, and Free Body Diagrams.
R1 / H1 · Connect (Laws) Law & Constraint Binding Connects physical laws (e.g. Newton's 2nd Law, Conservation of Energy, Stoichiometric Ratios).
R2 / H2 · Transform (Calculate) Algebraic Transformation Executes the algebraic manipulation, trigonometric substitutions, and numerical arithmetic.
R3 / H3 · Check (Verify) Independent Verification Dimensional analysis, extreme limit checks (e.g. m1 >> m2, θ = 0°), and symmetry checks.
R5 · Mastery (Crux) Competitive Mastery Resolves advanced multivariable optimization, non-inertial frames, and Olympiad twists.
