Diophantine Factorization Proof:
Equating slopes yields the classic intercept relation: \[ -\frac{b}{a} = \frac{1}{1 - a} \implies ab - a - b = 0 \iff (a - 1)(b - 1) = 1 \] When constrained to positive integers \( a, b \in \mathbb{Z}^+ \): \[ a - 1 = 1 \implies a = 2, \quad b - 1 = 1 \implies b = 2. \] Hence \( (2, 2) \) is the unique positive integer pair!
