Attempt first

A block moving on a rough horizontal surface stops after losing its kinetic energy to friction. The same surface is then inclined at 30 degrees and the block is projected up the incline with the same initial speed. Judge these claims: (1) the energy dissipated by friction before stopping is smaller on the incline; (2) the coefficient of friction becomes smaller when the plane is inclined.

Conditions

  • Same pair of surfaces is used.
  • Initial speed is the same in both trials.
  • Incline angle is 30 degrees.

Common wrong route

Assuming a smaller friction force means the coefficient itself has changed.
