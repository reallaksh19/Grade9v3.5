Target Concept ($X$)Frictionless inclined plane model with uniform gravity $g$

Attempt First

A block of mass $m$ slides from rest down a smooth, frictionless inclined plane of angle $\theta$ over a distance $s$ along the incline. Derive the general expression for its final speed $v$ at the bottom, and calculate $v$ when $s = 10\text{ m}$, $\theta = 30^\circ$, and $g = 9.8\text{ m/s}^2$.

Conditions & Stated Givens

  • Incline angle $\theta = 30^\circ$.
  • Frictionless surface (coefficient of friction $\mu = 0$).
  • Initial state: released from rest ($u = 0\text{ m/s}$).
  • Acceleration due to gravity $g = 9.8\text{ m/s}^2$.

⚠️ Common Wrong Route

Confusing ramp length $s$ with vertical height $h$ (forgetting the factor $\sin\theta$), or incorrectly assuming normal force performs work.

      Attempt First

      Given that $p - q = 9$, prove rigorously that $p^3 - q^3 - 27pq = 729$.

      Conditions & Stated Givens

      • Variables $p$ and $q$ are real numbers.
      • Linear constraint: $p - q = 9$.

      ⚠️ Common Wrong Route

      Attempting to substitute $p = q + 9$ into $p^3 - q^3$ and expanding by brute force, which leads to arithmetic confusion and sign errors.
