- Mixed work Q43 requires *infer productivity of two different worker types* from the two independent crew constraints, whereas Q36 provides two individual rates implicitly through their solo completion durations and 2025 Q38 assumes equal productivity and a fixed total worker-hour budget. - Two-time age Q43's stated audit epochs are **one year ago** and **four years from now**, five years apart, not four or six. B03-012 uses prior ages `6k,7k` and future `6k+5,7k+5`, then recovers the present age **36**. The authored #306 exercise with different epochs is a **new task** (legitimately different numbers), not an item-level transcription.
- Partnerships Q39 require separate time exposures **A 147000**, **B 189000**, **C 126000 rupee-months**; the source mathematical audit asks for the *difference between B and C's shares*, not necessarily one partner's complete standalone share. The conceptual foundation is capital-time aggregation, with a separate final comparison in the source crux.
- 2025 A Q30's B01 audited result is ₹4,965 of **compound interest**, **not compound amount**: `15000*((1.1)^3−1)`. The source transfers only the rate calculated from earlier simple interest, not accrued rupees.
- 2024 B Q19's original audit treats **the numeric annual percentage R equal to time t in years** and a **positive** solution `R=10/3` percent (3 1/3%), explicitly avoiding the physical-unit equality fallacy.

**Boundaries in general reuse:** Q37 conditional-subgroup percentages and 2025 Q40 successive percentages need different denominators in distinct ways; the first changes *population group*, the second changes *price base after a step*.
