## Specific visual/formula distinctions to preserve

Cone volume with the same radius permits cancellation of `πr²/3` and comparison of heights; curved cone area uses `πrℓ` with **slant** length. A sphere's surface grows by **radius squared**, unlike its volume; similar triangle area grows by **the square** of uniform side ratio. A hemispherical coating cost depends on selecting **curved** versus **total** area, `1 m² = 10,000 cm²`, and applying the quoted price unit. A conical shelter calculation keeps its floor *area* separate from enclosed air *volume*. An altitude is measured relative to the **chosen base**, not a visually horizontal default.

These are candidate source-specific inferences. A correct arithmetic match cannot substitute for authentic seven-component source readback, durable PDF bytes, official answer-key custody, independent academic acceptance, QRT cell review, rights and Owner release.
