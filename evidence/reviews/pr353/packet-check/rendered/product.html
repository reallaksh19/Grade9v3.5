Two equal masses m lie on a smooth horizontal surface and are joined by a light string of total length 2a. The midpoint of the initially taut string is pulled vertically upward by a small constant force F. When the masses are separated by 2x, determine the magnitude of the acceleration of either mass.

Conditions

  • Horizontal surface is frictionless.
  • String is light and remains taut.
  • Each half-string has length a.

Hints from the source

  1. How does the vertical displacement relate to half-string length and half-separation?
