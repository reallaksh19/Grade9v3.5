Resolve conflict between a local Lewis inventory and supplied aligned delocalisation.

Hard concept

Conditions for this construction

  • Can recall simple sp/sp2/sp3 labels.
  • Can distinguish sigma and pi overlap.
  • Knows electron-domain versus molecular geometry.
  • Knows introductory resonance contributors.
Why this section matters for the question set

    Result: A four-entry Lewis ledger does not settle the electronic model.
  2. The step the hard question turns onChoose a basis that represents both jobs.
    Why valid: Three in-plane sp2-like sigma directions can coexist with an aligned p-like lone-pair donor under the supplied model.
    Result: A planar sigma framework plus a p-like donor connected to the carbonyl pi space.
  3. Explain why automatic sp3 assignment is inadequate.
    Why valid: A localized tetrahedral rule omits the stated delocalisation and alignment conditions.
    Result: The local count is a useful ledger, not an overriding proof of the basis.
  4. Bound and counterfactually test the description.
    Why valid: Twisting or interrupting the donor-acceptor path changes the applicability; geometry alone does not uniquely measure hybrid coefficients.
    Result: An introductory sp2-like account is conditional, not unique observed orbital reality.

The key step

Given factors at 0,60,90 degrees are 1,1/2,0. Both cosine and cosine-squared are even. A degenerate two-orbital Hamiltonian with coupling t0 cos(theta) gives epsilon ± |t0 cos(theta)|, so no universal cosine-squared total-energy law follows.

A mathematical relation is evidence only about the quantity it defines. At 0°, 60° and 90° the supplied overlap-factor values are 1, 1/2 and 0; a total-energy law requires an additional energetic model or evidence.

  • Use the relation only for the overlap factor named in Q9.
  • Keep atom connectivity and formal electron count distinct from orbital orientation in the torsion comparison.
  • Do not infer donation magnitude or total molecular energy without an additional specified relation/model.
