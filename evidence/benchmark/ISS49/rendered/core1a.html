Formula reference · meaning and conditions

Equations and validity

EquationWhat it tells youWhen you can use it

p(x)=a_n x^n+...+a_1 x+a_0

A one-variable real-coefficient polynomial is a finite sum whose variable exponents are non-negative integers; missing powers have coefficient zero.

  • Only finitely many coefficients are non-zero.
  • The highest non-zero exponent is the degree for a non-zero polynomial.

Worked explanation

For 2x^4+3x-1, create a coefficient row for powers 4 through 0 and reconstruct the polynomial.

Follow the operation, its justification and its result.

  1. The row is 2,0,0,3,-1; reconstructing gives 2x^4+0x^3+0x^2+3x-1.

Result

The row is 2,0,0,3,-1; reconstructing gives 2x^4+0x^3+0x^2+3x-1.

Check

Every source term returns to the same exponent after reverse translation.

Treat coefficients as indexed by exponent slots. A missing written term contributes coefficient 0 without changing the positions of later coefficients.

Mistake clinic · diagnose and repair

A tempting wrong path

  • Missing powers can be deleted from a positional coefficient representation.

Diagnose

  • Write all coefficient slots for 2x^4+3x-1, including powers that do not appear.

Repair

  • Keep every exponent slot. Missing x^3 and x^2 terms have coefficient 0, so the row is 2,0,0,3,-1.

The key step

Preserve structure before simplifying: bracket substitutions, align like powers, and expose all products before collecting or applying an identity.

Formula reference · meaning and conditions

Equations and validity

EquationWhat it tells youWhen you can use it

a(b+c)=ab+ac

Multiplication distributes over addition; applying it repeatedly expands polynomial products.

  • Ordinary real-number algebra applies.

a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b)

Conjugate factors expand with cancelling cross terms.

  • The expression is a difference of two squares.

Worked explanation

A learner claims (x+2)^2=x^2+4. Expand from the product and identify what was omitted.

Follow the operation, its justification and its result.

  1. (x+2)(x+2)=x^2+2x+2x+4=x^2+4x+4; the two cross-products were omitted.

Result

(x+2)(x+2)=x^2+2x+2x+4=x^2+4x+4; the two cross-products were omitted.

Check

At x=1, both the square and corrected expansion equal 9.

Preserve structure before simplifying: bracket substitutions, align like powers, and expose all products before collecting or applying an identity.

Mistake clinic · diagnose and repair

A tempting wrong path

  • A binomial square can be formed by squaring only the two visible terms.

Diagnose

  • Expand (x+2)(x+2) as four products before combining anything.

Repair

  • The two cross-products are both 2x, so (x+2)^2=x^2+4x+4.

Equations and validity

EquationWhat it tells youWhen you can use it

Rectangle area is the product of perpendicular side lengths.

a(b+c)=ab+ac

Area=(x+4)(x+2)=x^2+6x+8 cm^2.

Conditions for this construction

  • Substitution into a familiar linear expression and signed-number operations are available.
  • Interpreting a polynomial zero and coordinating coefficient constraints are not assumed secure.
Why this section matters for the question set

This unit builds toward

  • Q10 · D2 · the hardest question in this set
    The move learners miss: Translate 'zero is 1' into p(1)=0 and solve for a. Try it in Core2
  1. Write the constrained family.
    Why valid: A fixed constant b leaves p(x)=ax+b with a as the remaining coefficient for a linear polynomial.
    Result: one-parameter family
  2. The step the hard question turns onTranslate the zero into an equation.
    Why valid: r is a zero exactly when p(r)=0.
    Result: equation ar+b=0
  3. Solve the parameter equation.
    Why valid: Signed-number operations determine the coefficient when r is non-zero.
    Result: parameter fixed
  4. Recheck both conditions and remaining freedom.
    Why valid: The constant and zero must both hold; a single forced coefficient establishes uniqueness.
    Result: verified unique polynomial

The key step

Represent all polynomials satisfying the fixed constant first, then turn the specified zero into an equation for the only remaining coefficient; uniqueness follows only after that freedom is exhausted.

Formula reference · meaning and conditions

Equations and validity

EquationWhat it tells youWhen you can use it

r is a zero of p iff p(r)=0

A specified zero is a substitution condition on the polynomial.

  • r is in the polynomial's real input domain.

Worked explanation

Construct a linear polynomial with constant term 6 and zero 3, then decide whether more than one can satisfy both conditions.

Follow the operation, its justification and its result.

  1. p(x)=-2x+6, uniquely, because 3a+6=0 forces a=-2.

Result

p(x)=-2x+6, uniquely, because 3a+6=0 forces a=-2.

Check

The constant is 6 and p(3)=0; no free coefficient remains.

Represent all polynomials satisfying the fixed constant first, then turn the specified zero into an equation for the only remaining coefficient; uniqueness follows only after that freedom is exhausted.

Mistake clinic · diagnose and repair

A tempting wrong path

  • Any non-zero scaling preserves both a zero and a fixed constant term.

Diagnose

  • If p(x)=ax+6 must have zero 3, does multiplying one solution by 5 keep the constant term equal to 6?

Repair

  • Scaling preserves zeros but scales the constant too. Use 3a+6=0 to fix a directly.

  2. 2 · ApplyConstruct a linear polynomial with constant term -4 and zero 2, and justify whether it is unique.
  3. 3 · ConnectState which invariant or defining condition made the method valid.
Question-specific diagnosis and repair

Repair for Q10

Test the rule on a changed case

Construct a linear polynomial with constant term 6 and zero 3. Before comparing, state whether the coefficient of x is still free after both conditions are imposed.

Expected pattern: p(x)=ax+6, p(3)=0 gives 3a+6=0, so a=-2; the coefficient is no longer free and the polynomial is unique.

This pattern suggests a rule to revisit; it does not diagnose you or certify mastery.

Replacement rule: For p(x)=ax+b with fixed non-zero b and specified non-zero zero r, ar+b=0 determines a uniquely; both conditions must be rechecked.

Fresh application: Construct a linear polynomial with constant term -4 and zero 2, and justify uniqueness.
