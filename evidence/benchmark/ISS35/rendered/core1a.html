Q2 · Owner-supplied question, verbatim

Use the supplied nitrate description: three planar sigma bond directions around nitrogen and a pi system delocalised across the N–O framework. Translate the three Lewis contributors into one qualitative orbital diagram. Distinguish the sigma framework from the delocalised pi system, and explain what is preserved and what must not be interpreted as a molecule switching between drawings.

Before opening each step, say what you would do next.

  1. Predict step 1, then reveal
    Compare all contributors and classify invariant versus contributor-specific features.

    Why valid: Translation begins from preserved meaning rather than copied line positions.

    Result: invariant/contributor-local split

  2. Predict step 2, then reveal
    Fix the common sigma framework as three coplanar N-O directions.

    Why valid: The benchmark supplies one planar three-direction sigma framework.

    Result: one planar sigma skeleton

  3. Predict step 3, then reveal
    Place the relevant p orbitals perpendicular to that plane.

    Why valid: Side-on pi overlap requires p-orbital direction distinct from the in-plane sigma framework.

    Result: multi-centre p-orbital set

  4. Predict step 4, then reveal
    Represent the pi information as one delocalised system; treat Lewis drawings as formal contributors.

    Why valid: This preserves equivalence without inventing time switching.

    Result: one delocalised orbital description

Result and independent check

Result

Keep one planar three-direction N-O sigma framework. Place the relevant p orbitals perpendicular to that plane and represent pi electron density as delocalised over the N-O framework. Connectivity, total charge/electron inventory and the sigma skeleton are invariant; formal pi-bond/charge placements are contributor-local. The contributors are formal components, not structures the ion switches between in time.

Check

The orbital description must translate back to three equivalent N-O positions rather than selecting one permanent N=O.

Separate the invariant sigma framework from contributor-local formal bond/charge placement, then build one delocalised pi model without turning resonance drawings into time states.

Diagnose

Repair

  • Treat contributors as formal components; preserve one sigma framework and translate pi information into one delocalised description.

Watch one · predict before reveal

Q3 · Owner-supplied question, verbatim

For propadiene (allene), H2C=C=CH2, apply local electron-domain counting at each of the three carbon atoms. Assign the introductory hybridisation of each carbon and show how the two mutually perpendicular unhybridised p orbitals on the central carbon constrain the relative planes of the terminal CH2 groups.

Before opening each step, say what you would do next.

  1. Predict step 1, then reveal
    Count sigma directions at each carbon separately.

    Why valid: Hybridisation is atom-local in this introductory model.

    Result: three local centres

  2. Predict step 2, then reveal
    Assign terminal sp2 and central sp from local sigma directions.

    Why valid: A multiple bond contributes one sigma direction; sp leaves two p orbitals and sp2 leaves one.

    Result: sp2-sp-sp2

  3. Predict step 3, then reveal
    Use the two mutually perpendicular central p orbitals for the two separate pi overlaps.

    Why valid: The two unhybridised p orbitals on an sp centre are orthogonal.

    Result: two orthogonal pi systems

  4. Predict step 4, then reveal
    Propagate those p-axis directions to the terminal p orbitals and CH2 planes.

    Why valid: Each terminal p must align with its central partner; its sp2 sigma plane is perpendicular to that p orbital.

    Result: terminal CH2 planes perpendicular

Result and independent check

Result

Each terminal carbon has three sigma directions and is described as sp2 with one unhybridised p orbital. The central carbon has two sigma directions and is described as sp, leaving two mutually perpendicular unhybridised p orbitals. Each central p orbital overlaps with a terminal p orbital, so the two terminal p axes and terminal CH2 planes are mutually perpendicular.

Check

Looking down the C=C=C axis, the two terminal CH2 groups should project at about 90 degrees in this introductory orbital model.

After local sigma-framework counting, use the number and direction of remaining unhybridised p orbitals as a consistency check on the three-dimensional pi system.

Mistake clinic · diagnose and repair

A tempting wrong path

  • Flatten allene into an ethene-like plane or assume separated double bonds are automatically conjugated.

Diagnose

  • How many distinct central p orbitals are required in allene, or is there a p orbital on every bridge atom in the diene?

Repair

  • Use local p-orbital inventory and orientation/adjacency checks before inferring geometry or conjugation.

Q10 · Owner-supplied question, verbatim

A student assigns one hybridisation label to an entire molecule. Use propyne and allene to test this procedure and devise a more reliable local procedure. State the electron-domain information and orbital-consistency checks your procedure needs.

Before opening each step, say what you would do next.

  1. Predict step 1, then reveal
    Reject molecule-wide labeling by testing propyne and allene atom by atom.

    Why valid: Each molecule contains nonequivalent carbon centres with different local sigma environments.

    Result: one-label procedure fails

  2. Predict step 2, then reveal
    For each atom count local sigma directions and lone-pair domains; a multiple bond contributes one sigma direction.

    Why valid: The introductory model describes local sigma geometry.

    Result: local domain inventory

  3. Predict step 3, then reveal
    Map two, three or four local domains to sp, sp2 or sp3.

    Why valid: These labels describe digonal, trigonal-planar or tetrahedral local sigma frameworks in the simplified model.

    Result: local assignment

  4. Predict step 4, then reveal
    Check leftover unhybridised p orbitals against every required pi bond/delocalisation and orientation.

    Why valid: A local label that cannot support the required pi system is internally inconsistent.

    Result: orbital-consistent procedure

Result and independent check

Result

A molecule-wide label fails: propyne contains an sp3 methyl carbon and two sp alkyne carbons; allene contains two terminal sp2 carbons and an sp central carbon. A better procedure is atom-local: for each atom, count sigma-bond directions and lone-pair electron domains (a multiple bond is one sigma direction), assign the local introductory geometry/hybridisation, then check that enough correctly oriented unhybridised p orbitals remain for all required pi bonds/delocalisation. Revisit an assignment that fails that orbital check.

Check

The procedure must simultaneously return propyne sp3/sp/sp and allene sp2/sp/sp2 while leaving the correct number and orientation of p orbitals.

Treat hybridisation as an atom-local sigma-framework model, then verify that enough correctly oriented unhybridised p orbitals remain for the required pi system.

Mistake clinic · diagnose and repair

A tempting wrong path

  • Assign one hybridisation label to an entire molecule, infer sp2 from equivalent bonds, or count a triple bond as three local domains.

Diagnose

  • What are the separate domain counts at each nonequivalent atom, and do the resulting labels leave the required p orbitals?

Repair

  • Assign locally from the sigma framework, then run an independent p-orbital count/orientation check.
