The Universal Double-Face Subtraction Law

Mathematical Invariant

When any two solids $A$ and $B$ are glued together along a matching boundary of area $A_{\text{joint}}$, both solids lose their contact face from the outside world.

$$\mathbf{\text{Composite Exposed Area} = \text{TSA}_A + \text{TSA}_B - 2 \cdot A_{\text{joint}}}$$

For our toy: $(\pi r l + \pi r^2) + (3\pi r^2) - 2(\pi r^2) = \pi r l + 2\pi r^2$. Each joint eliminates two previously exposed circular faces!

In the assembled toy, this circular face is trapped inside the joint! No paint or air can touch it. In mensuration, internal faces never count toward surface area.

Live Mathematical Synthesis

Step-by-Step Ledger
Step 1 · Auxiliary Pythagorean Bridge ($l$)
$$l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2} = \sqrt{3.5^2 + 12^2} = \sqrt{156.25} = \mathbf{12.5\text{ cm}}$$
Step 2 · Exposed Components
$$\text{Cone CSA} = \pi r l = \frac{22}{7} \times 3.5 \times 12.5 = \mathbf{137.5\text{ cm}^2}$$ $$\text{Hemisphere CSA} = 2\pi r^2 = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times (3.5)^2 = \mathbf{77.0\text{ cm}^2}$$
Step 3 · Total Exposed Surface Area
$$\text{Exposed Area} = \pi r(l + 2r) = 137.5 + 77.0 = \mathbf{214.5\text{ cm}^2}$$
