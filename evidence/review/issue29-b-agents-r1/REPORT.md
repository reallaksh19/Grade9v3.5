## 3. Findings that block golden promotion

### F01 — Correct answers can contain incorrect warrants and checks

**#32 Q4:** summary requests three NH bonds plus a lone pair, but MOVE-2 asks for two in-plane bonds, a wedge, a dash and a lone pair: five directions. Its chosen tetrahedral SVG actually draws methane, C with four H, without an N/lone-pair construction. The demanded NH3 labelled representation is missing. Correct the route and create a molecule-specific view or a validated parameterized view.

**#36 Q4:** the correct allene perpendicular-plane conclusion is justified by claiming the central carbon's first p orbital is “already fully occupied” in a bond, making its reuse forbidden by Pauli exclusion. That is not a valid atomic-orbital occupation argument; orbitals can participate in multicentre delocalized descriptions, which the same candidate teaches for nitrate. Explain the two perpendicular central p directions and the terminal sigma-plane constraints within the stated model. An on-atom orthogonality identity alone is not a substitute for specifying an inter-centre overlap and its geometry.

**#36 Q7:** four perpendicular p orbitals in carbonate span a six-electron pi space, not a system holding only one pi electron pair. Count one C=O pi pair and the two singly bonded oxygens' perpendicular lone-pair contributions in a Lewis contributor. Its `-2/3` on each O is the equal-contributor average of formal-charge bookkeeping, not a directly observed unique atomic partial charge. Equal bonds do not make formal charge into measured charge. Correct the answer, repeated move output and teaching/reference material together.

**#36 Q8:** the central sigma bond can persist through ethane rotation in the elementary picture. The added claims “no bonding orbital overlap is altered” throughout the molecule and “purely ... steric repulsion” overreach. Torsion also changes hyperconjugative interactions; an introductory axial diagram does not establish the whole electronic energy decomposition. Keep the elementary sigma/pi contrast and label its limits.

**#38 Q2:** formamide `CH3NO` has **18 valence electrons**, although it has **24 total electrons**. The answer's check calls 24 valence electrons. Its pi subsystem in the supplied model has four electrons; preserve the distinction among all electrons, valence electrons and the chosen pi subsystem.

**#38 Q9:** factors `1, 1/2, 0` and rejection of a total-energy inference are correct. Its supposedly independent check says `E(theta)=E(-theta)` favors cosine squared because cosine is asymmetric. **Cosine is even:** `cos(-theta)=cos(theta)`. Also, second-order weak-coupling stabilization can scale with interaction squared under specified assumptions; Hückel theory does not universally require that law. A two-degenerate-orbital Hamiltonian with coupling `t0 cos(theta)` has energies `epsilon +/- |t0 cos(theta)|`. This counterexample refutes the universal assertion without claiming to predict formamide's energy.

The full [40-question ledger](question-audit.md) distinguishes correct core answers, usable answers with overclaims, and substantive failures. Those statuses are deliberately narrower than golden acceptance. Generic scientific polish, an author's VERIFIED_CORRECT label, or a correct final shape must not conceal a wrong reason.

### F06 — Builder proposals need their own science and feasibility check

**#32:** a wedge/dash construction tool could help, but a notation choice is not automatically a quantitative electrostatic energy calculation. Separate “decode/construct 3D directions” from any explicitly specified toy repulsion model. Give it an actual NH3/CH4 domain configuration, not a generic mislabeled asset.

**#34:** a 3D orbital inspector is potentially useful, but the proposal adds terminal-oxygen/lone-pair-plane tasks not required by Q4 and describes learner struggles as if observed without learner-study evidence. Treat these as hypotheses. Label localized CO2 accounting as an approximation; do not grade a unique oxygen-plane orientation as experimentally mandatory.

**#36:** the proposed `Overlap(theta)=|cos(theta)|` says zero at 0 degrees and one at 90 degrees. The formula gives one and zero. Terminal-CH2 dihedral and p-axis misalignment need distinct definitions; use a shifted relation when modelling the stated allene endpoints. Its “30 lines” claim is an estimate, not evidence of implemented accessible 3D behavior.

**#38:** a continuous parameter comparator is the most generally reusable idea. The proposed SVG attribute `transform="rotate(var(--g9-scrub-angle, 0deg) ...)"` is not valid SVG transform-attribute syntax; a CSS custom property there does not implement the promised rotation. Use a numeric JS-set SVG transform or an explicitly tested CSS transform. Rotation about a molecular bond is not merely rotating the whole 2D picture. No-JS stage selection is also not established by promising that script-dependent stage chips “continue to function unchanged.” Specify a real static fallback.

Recommended module target: **qualitative orbital-alignment comparator**, with declared molecular axis, angle convention, model relation, orbital phase/symmetry convention, sigma-framework invariant, electron-count invariant, and explicit “not total energy” boundary. At named states and an intermediate state, compare learner prediction to the actual visual geometry and model output. In Core1A it supports construction and later transfer; in Core2 it must preserve the question's learner-owned decision. It stays an optional enhancement within the existing Core2 reader. This audit does not authorize coding or certify any proposal as implemented.
