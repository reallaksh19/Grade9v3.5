Physics D3 (Score 6) Selected D3/D4

A block takes time t to slide from rest down a 45° inclined plane. When the same plane is made frictionless, the time becomes t/2. If the original coefficient of friction is alpha/100, find alpha.

REPRESENT: Write the down-slope acceleration once with friction and once without friction.
CONNECT: For equal distance from rest, compare times through s=(1/2)at^2 rather than through speed.
✍️ Your Working & Answer Commit
Reveal Checked Solution & Crux Move

Verified Answer: 75.

Reasoning Route:

• For the rough 45° plane, a_r=g(sin45°-mu cos45°)=g(1-mu)/sqrt(2). For the smooth plane, a_s=g/sqrt(2).

• For the same distance from rest, time is proportional to 1/sqrt(a). Since the smooth time is t/2, a_s=4a_r.

• Therefore 1=4(1-mu), giving mu=3/4=75/100 and alpha=75.

Crux Move: Same-distance motion from rest makes t proportional to 1/sqrt(a), so t/2 means four times the acceleration.
Watch Out (Common Wrong Route): Assuming halving the time only doubles acceleration, or omitting the normal-force factor in kinetic friction.
