⚡ Core Bonding Rules

• Bohr-Bury Max Shell Capacity: 2n² (K=2, L=8, M=18, N=32)
• Octet Rule: Outermost shell max 8 electrons.
• Duplet Rule: H, He stable with 2 electrons.
• Valency = Valence e⁻ (if ≤ 4) OR (8 - Valence e⁻) (if > 4).
• Criss-Cross Formula Rule: Cation charge & Anion charge cross to form neutral unit.
