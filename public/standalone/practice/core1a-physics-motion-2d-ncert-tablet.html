🎯 2D Motion NCERT Revised Core Instances (19)

Curated NCERT problems spanning curved path distance-displacement ratios, average speed vs average velocity, and circular trajectories.

NCERT 2D Page 2
M2D-NC-01 Half-circle displacement

A particle travels from one end of a diameter to the opposite end along a semicircular path of radius r. What is the magnitude of its displacement?

👁️ Reveal Worked Derivation
The displacement magnitude is 2r, the straight-line diameter joining the endpoints. The semicircle arc length is distance travelled, not displacement.
NCERT 2D Page 3
M2D-NC-02 Constant-speed merry-go-round

A rider moves around a merry-go-round at constant speed. Is the rider's velocity constant? State the direction of the acceleration.

👁️ Reveal Worked Derivation
No. The speed is constant but the velocity direction changes continuously. The acceleration is centripetal: it points toward the centre.
NCERT 2D Page 4
M2D-NC-03 Distance versus displacement

When can the distance travelled equal the magnitude of displacement?

👁️ Reveal Worked Derivation
When the motion is along a straight line in one direction without reversal; then the path length equals the start-to-finish separation.
NCERT 2D Page 5
M2D-NC-04 String breaks in circular motion

A stone moves in a horizontal circle on a string. If the string suddenly breaks, in what direction does the stone move immediately after release?

👁️ Reveal Worked Derivation
It moves along the tangent to the circle at the release point, in the direction of its instantaneous velocity.
NCERT 2D Page 6
M2D-NC-05 What keeps a planet turning?

What provides the centripetal force for a planet revolving around the Sun, and what is the direction of its acceleration?

👁️ Reveal Worked Derivation
The Sun's gravitational force provides the centripetal force. The planet's acceleration points toward the Sun.
NCERT 2D Page 7
M2D-NC-06 Horizontal throw versus drop

Two stones start simultaneously from the same height. One is dropped and the other is thrown horizontally. Neglect air resistance. Which reaches the ground first?

👁️ Reveal Worked Derivation
They reach the ground at the same time because their vertical motions start with the same vertical velocity (zero) and have the same acceleration g.
NCERT 2D Page 8
M2D-NC-07 Moon if Earth gravity vanished

Suppose Earth's gravitational pull on the Moon suddenly became zero and no other celestial body affected the Moon. What path would the Moon follow immediately afterward?

👁️ Reveal Worked Derivation
It would move in a straight line tangent to its former orbit with its instantaneous velocity.
NCERT 2D Page 9
M2D-NC-08 Why Earth does not fall into the Sun

The Sun attracts Earth gravitationally. Why does Earth not simply fall straight into the Sun?

👁️ Reveal Worked Derivation
Earth has tangential velocity. Gravity continually bends that velocity toward the Sun, producing orbital motion rather than a straight radial fall.
NCERT 2D Page 10
M2D-NC-09 Coin tossed inside a train

A passenger tosses a coin vertically inside a moving train and the coin lands behind the passenger. What does that imply about the train's motion while the coin was airborne?

👁️ Reveal Worked Derivation
The train accelerated forward while the coin was airborne; after release, the coin did not share the train's continuing forward acceleration.
