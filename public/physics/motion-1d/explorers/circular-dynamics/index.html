Start with motion, not force names

Reasoning order

  1. Speed can stay constant while velocity direction changes.
  2. Changing velocity means acceleration is nonzero.
  3. For uniform circular motion that acceleration is inward: ar=v²/r.
  4. Only then ask which real interactions supply the required inward net force.
Invariant: tangent velocity and inward acceleration are perpendicular in the uniform case.
