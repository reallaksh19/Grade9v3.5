📑 Key Takeaways
1 N12 is not equal to the applied force F.
2 The contact force depends on which mass must be accelerated.
3 Reversing the push direction changes the contact force, even though the system acceleration is the same.
4 Action-reaction pairs act on different objects and only cancel when both objects are in the system.

Free-Body Diagrams: Isolated Block Analysis

Isolate each block to reveal the exact force vectors. Observe how Newton's Third Law pairs appear on separate bodies.

◉ Block 1 Isolated (m1)
Horizontal Balance:
ΣFx = F − N21 = m1a
N21 opposes F, reducing net acceleration of Block 1.
◉ Block 2 Isolated (m2)
Horizontal Balance:
ΣFx = N12 = m2a
N12 is the ONLY horizontal force driving Block 2 forward!
◉ Combined System (m1 + m2)
Internal Cancellation:
F − N21 + N12 = (m1 + m2)a
Since |N12| = |N21|, they cancel internally: F = Mtota!

Microscopic View: Atomic Origin of Normal Contact Force

At the microscopic interface, atoms undergo elastic compression. Inter-atomic electromagnetic repulsion enforces |N12| ≡ |N21|.

Why Action-Reaction Pairs Never Cancel on One Body:
A common misconception is: "If N₁₂ and N₂₁ are equal and opposite, why do the blocks move at all?"
Because they act on different bodies! N12 acts exclusively on Block 2, while N21 acts exclusively on Block 1. Forces can only cancel if they act on the same object.
Universal Third Law Invariance:
Newton's Third Law is an instantaneous invariant. It does NOT depend on whether the blocks are accelerating, moving at constant velocity, or at rest.
|N12| ≡ |N21|   (Always and Everywhere)
