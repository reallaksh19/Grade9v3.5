JEE Main 2026 (Online) 22nd January Morning Shift • Velocity Invariant at Angle 45° and at the Apex (θ₀ = 60°)

A projectile is thrown upward at an angle of 60° with the horizontal with initial speed u. Find the speed of the projectile when its direction makes an angle of 45° with the horizontal, and find its speed at the highest point (apex).

ExamSide ↗
View Full Step-by-Step Teacher Solution
Expand ↓ Collapse ↑
• 1. Core Invariant & Free-Body Audit
Audit: Throughout the flight, aₓ = 0. Therefore, the horizontal component of velocity is strictly conserved: vₓ(t) = u cos 60° = u / 2 = constant everywhere.
• 2. Symbolic Derivation & Decoupled Equations
1. Speed when direction is 45°:
Direction angle is given by tan 45° = vᵢ / vₓ = 1 ⇒ vᵢ = vₓ = u / 2.
Total speed v = √(vₓ² + vᵢ²) = √((u/2)² + (u/2)²) = u / √2 ≈ 0.707 u.
2. Speed at the Highest Point (Apex):
At the apex, vertical velocity vanishes momentarily (vᵢ = 0).
Therefore, total velocity at apex is strictly horizontal: v_apex = vₓ = u cos 60° = u / 2.
• 3. Numerical Substitution & Verification
Acceleration at Apex: While vᵢ = 0, acceleration is aᵢ = −g ≠ 0. If acceleration were zero at the apex, the projectile would hover in mid-air indefinitely! Continuous gravity pulls Δvᵢ = −g Δt, initiating immediate descent.
⚠ 4. JEE Examiner Trap Alert
Common Trap: Choosing v = 0 at the highest point. Only the vertical component vᵢ is zero. The horizontal component vₓ remains completely intact and unaffected by gravity.

JEE Main 2026 (Online) 24th January Morning Shift • Ball Thrown at 45° Lands on Building Roof: Find Building Height h

A boy throws a ball into air at 45° from the horizontal to land it on a roof of a building of height h. If the ball attains maximum height in 2 s and lands on the building in 3 s after launch, find the value of h.

ExamSide ↗

Expand ↓ Collapse ↑
• 1. Core Invariant & Free-Body Audit
Audit: Apex milestone occurs when vertical velocity vᵢ = 0. Landing milestone occurs at an elevated platform Δy = h ≠ 0 at time t_land = 3 s. Naive ground-landing formulas cannot be applied.
• 2. Symbolic Derivation & Decoupled Equations
1. Vertical Launch Speed from Apex Clock:
At apex (t = 2 s): vᵢ = v₀ᵢ − g t_apex = 0 ⇒ v₀ᵢ = g · t_apex = 10 × 2 = 20 m/s.
Since launch angle is 45°, v₀ₓ = v₀ᵢ = 20 m/s.
2. Elevation at Landing (t = 3 s):
h = y(3) = v₀ᵢ t_land − ½ g t_land²
h = 20(3) − ½ (10) (3)² = 60 − 45 = 15 m.
Teacher's Geometric Alternative: Max height H_max = ½ g (2)² = 20 m. Free-fall descent from apex for Δt = 3 − 2 = 1 s gives fall distance Δy_fall = ½ (10) (1)² = 5 m. Therefore, roof height h = H_max − Δy_fall = 20 − 5 = 15 m!
• 3. Numerical Substitution & Verification
Summary Results: Roof height h = 15 m. Total max height reached above ground H_max = 20 m. Horizontal range to roof = v₀ₓ × 3 = 60 m.
⚠ 4. JEE Examiner Trap Alert
Common Trap: Assuming flight time is symmetrical (t_flight = 2 × t_apex = 4 s). Because the building roof is elevated at h = 15 m, landing occurs early at 3 s while the ball is descending!
