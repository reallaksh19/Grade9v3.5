THE EVENT TRANSFER PIPELINE

TRIGGER: X-AXIS
1 PHYSICAL CONSTRAINT (TRIGGER) HORIZONTAL

The projectile encounters the wall when its horizontal position reaches x = d.

x(t*) = d  ⇒  v₀ₓ × t* = d
2 SOLVE FOR "WHEN" (t*) EVENT TIME

The horizontal equation alone dictates the exact timestamp of arrival:

t* = d / v₀ₓ = 2.00 s
3 ONE SHARED CLOCK BROADCAST SYNCHRONIZED

Both coordinates sample the single event clock: tₓ ≡ tᵢ ≡ t*.

4 SAMPLE OPPOSITE AXIS ("WHERE / WHAT") VERTICAL

Evaluate vertical motion at this exact t* to find clearance height:

y(t*) = v₀ᵢt* − ½gt*² = 14.3 m

Crucial Insight: Horizontal Controls Arrival Time

Notice that when throwing a ball towards a vertical wall at distance d, gravity g and vertical speed have zero influence on when the ball hits the wall. Arrival time is strictly t* = d / v₀ₓ!
