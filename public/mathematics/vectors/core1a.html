Vector Resolution & Orthogonal Decomposition

Mathematical construction of Cartesian unit vectors (\(\hat{i}, \hat{j}, \hat{k}\)), trigonometric projection, quadrant sign integrity, and resultant reconstruction.

4-Step Vector Resolution Protocol

  1. Establish Reference Frame & Angle Orientation:
    Define the origin and orthogonal axes (\(x\) horizontal, \(y\) vertical). Identify the precise reference line for angle \(\theta\):
    $$\text{Adjacent side} = \text{Hypotenuse} \times \cos\theta, \quad \text{Opposite side} = \text{Hypotenuse} \times \sin\theta$$
    Rule: The component along the arm with which angle \(\theta\) is made takes the cosine; the perpendicular arm takes the sine.
  2. Assign Orthogonal Unit Vector Projections:
    Decompose the vector into signed rectangular components along unit vectors \(\hat{i}\) (along \(+x\)) and \(\hat{j}\) (along \(+y\)):
    $$\vec{A} = A_x \hat{i} + A_y \hat{j}$$
  3. Crux Step · The Quadrant Trap
    Verify Quadrant Sign Integrity:
    Never blindly assume components are positive. Match the algebraic sign of \(A_x\) and \(A_y\) to the spatial quadrant:
    • Quadrant I: \((+x, +y) \implies (+\hat{i}, +\hat{j})\)
    • Quadrant II: \((-x, +y) \implies (-\hat{i}, +\hat{j})\)
    • Quadrant III: \((-x, -y) \implies (-\hat{i}, -\hat{j})\)
    • Quadrant IV: \((+x, -y) \implies (+\hat{i}, -\hat{j})\)
  4. Recombine Multi-Vector Resultants:
    To add arbitrary vectors \(\vec{A} + \vec{B} + \vec{C}\), sum components independently:
    $$R_x = \sum A_x, \quad R_y = \sum A_y \implies |\vec{R}| = \sqrt{R_x^2 + R_y^2}$$
    Direction relative to \(+x\) is given by \(\alpha = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{R_y}{R_x}\right)\).

Watch One · Authentic Vector Resolution Exemplar

Problem: A force vector of magnitude \(20\text{ N}\) points \(30^\circ\) above the \(-x\) axis. Resolve it into Cartesian unit vector notation.

  1. Identify Orientation: The vector lies in Quadrant II. The angle \(\theta = 30^\circ\) is measured relative to the \(-x\) axis.
  2. Calculate Magnitudes:
    $$|F_x| = 20 \cos(30^\circ) = 20 \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 10\sqrt{3}\text{ N} \approx 17.32\text{ N}$$ $$|F_y| = 20 \sin(30^\circ) = 20 \times \frac{1}{2} = 10\text{ N}$$
  3. Apply Quadrant Signs (Crux Step): Since it points along the negative \(x\)-direction and positive \(y\)-direction:
    \(F_x = -10\sqrt{3}\text{ N}, \quad F_y = +10\text{ N}\).
  4. Vector Notation:
    $$\vec{F} = -10\sqrt{3}\hat{i} + 10\hat{j}\text{ N}$$
