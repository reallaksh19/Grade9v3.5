# AP-010 — Repeated hint rung

**Observed:** [review commit `9bf92e03`](https://github.com/reallaksh19/Grade9V3/commit/9bf92e03), COV-04; [review commit `1d012787`](https://github.com/reallaksh19/Grade9V3/commit/1d012787), SAME-HEIGHT-05. This card paraphrases the observed mechanism; its before/after sketches are teaching examples, not quotations from the review.

**Learner experience:** A later hint consumes attention without moving the learner forward.

**Mechanism:** Two rungs give the same orientation in different words.

**Before, pattern sketch:** Rung 2 repeats “draw the forces” from rung 1.

**After, repair sketch:** Make the next rung request a representation or first relation.

**Technique:** T05 in [TECHNIQUES.md](../TECHNIQUES.md).

**Structurally impossible now?** No: requires reading the sequence as a learner.
