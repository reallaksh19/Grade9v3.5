🎯 SBA-04: Range Optimization & Complementary Angles

When projection angles sum to 90° ($ heta$ and $90^\circ - heta$), horizontal ranges are strictly identical: $R( heta) = R(90^\circ - heta)$.

Product of Flights:    T₁ · T₂ = (2 / g) · R
Ratio of Heights:     H₁ / H₂ = tan² θ
Geometric Mean:       R = 4 √(H₁ · H₂)

🧭 SBA-05: Velocity Vector at Any Instant

At any time $t$ during flight, the velocity vector is $ ec{v}(t) = u_x \hat{i} + (u_y - gt) \hat{j}$.

Instantaneous Speed:    v = √(u_x² + (u_y - gt)²)
Angle with Horizontal: tan α = v_y / v_x = (u_y - gt) / u_x
Condition v ⊥ u:       v⃗ · u⃗ = 0  ⇒  t = u / (g sin θ)

When projected horizontally from height $h$ ($u_y = 0, u_x = u$):
