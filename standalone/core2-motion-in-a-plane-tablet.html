Inside a train accelerating uniformly along a straight track, a boy throws a ball forward at 10 m/s relative to the train and 60 degrees above the horizontal. To catch it again at the launch height he moves 1.15 m forward inside the train. Using g=10 m/s2s^{2}, find the train acceleration in m/s2s^{2}.

Conditions

  • Throw speed is relative to the train.
  • Train acceleration is constant.
  • Catch occurs at the original vertical level.

Common wrong route

Treating the train as constant-velocity and using only the ball's horizontal component.
