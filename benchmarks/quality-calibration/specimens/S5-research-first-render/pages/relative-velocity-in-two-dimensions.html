Relative velocity in two dimensions

Key idea

The velocity of A relative to B is v_A − v_B. In two dimensions, subtract the vectors component by component or with a triangle.

Understand it

An observer moving with velocity v_B sees every other object with its own velocity minus v_B. This is why rain that falls straight down appears slanted to a moving cyclist.

For a boat crossing a river, the ground velocity is the boat's velocity through the water plus the water's velocity; the two are perpendicular when the boat heads straight across.

Draw the vectors head to tail and use Pythagoras for the size and the tangent for the direction.

Common slip: Adding the speeds (4 + 3 = 7) ignores direction; perpendicular velocities combine by Pythagoras.

Worked example

A swimmer swims at 1.2 m/s straight across a river flowing at 0.5 m/s. Find the swimmer's speed relative to the bank.

  1. The swimmer's velocity through the water and the river's velocity are perpendicular.
  2. Combine them by Pythagoras: v = √(1.2² + 0.5²) = √(1.44 + 0.25) = 1.3 m/s.
  3. Direction: tan φ = 0.5 / 1.2, so the swimmer drifts about 23° downstream of straight across.

Result: 1.3 m/s, about 23° downstream.

Rebuild it yourself

Explain why rain falling straight down looks slanted to someone riding a bicycle.

Compare with a model answer

The cyclist sees the rain's velocity minus her own velocity. Subtracting her forward velocity adds a backward horizontal component to the rain's vertical velocity, so the rain appears to come towards her at a slant.

Practice

A boat moves at 4 m/s in still water. It heads straight across a river flowing at 3 m/s. Find the resultant speed of the boat relative to the bank.

Hint

The two velocities are perpendicular.

Worked answer
  1. v = √(4² + 3²).
  2. v = 5 m/s.

Answer: 5 m/s

Rain falls vertically at 6 m/s. A cyclist rides at 8 m/s. Find the speed of the rain relative to the cyclist.

Hint

Subtract the cyclist's velocity from the rain's velocity.

Worked answer
  1. v_rain − v_cyclist = (−8, −6) m/s.
  2. Relative speed = √(8² + 6²) = 10 m/s.

Answer: 10 m/s
