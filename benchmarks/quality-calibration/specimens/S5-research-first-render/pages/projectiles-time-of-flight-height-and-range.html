Projectiles: time of flight, height and range

Key idea

Split the launch velocity into u cos θ horizontally and u sin θ vertically. The vertical part decides the time and the height; the horizontal part, multiplied by the time, gives the range.

Understand it

A projectile launched at speed u and angle θ has uₓ = u cos θ, which stays constant, and u_y = u sin θ, which falls by g every second until it reaches zero at the top.

The time to the top is u sin θ / g, so on level ground the time of flight is T = 2u sin θ / g. The maximum height is H = (u sin θ)² / 2g.

The range is the horizontal velocity multiplied by the time of flight: R = u cos θ × T = u² sin 2θ / g, which is largest at 45°.

Common slip: At the top the velocity is not zero; only the vertical component is zero, and the horizontal component continues.

Worked example

A ball is kicked at 10 m/s at 30° above level ground. Find the time of flight and the range. Take g = 10 m/s².

  1. Split the launch velocity: u_y = 10 × sin 30° = 5 m/s and uₓ = 10 × cos 30° ≈ 8.66 m/s.
  2. Time of flight from the vertical motion: T = 2u_y / g = 2 × 5 / 10 = 1 s.
  3. Range from the horizontal motion using the same time: R = 8.66 × 1 ≈ 8.7 m.

Result: 1 s and about 8.7 m.

Rebuild it yourself

Explain why the range on level ground is the same for launch angles of 30° and 60° at the same speed.

Compare with a model answer

Range is u² sin 2θ / g. For 30° and 60°, 2θ is 60° and 120°, which have the same sine. The steeper launch stays up longer but moves sideways more slowly, and the two effects balance.

Practice

Source: Fixture Source Paper A (2020), Q8

A ball is thrown with speed 20 m/s at 30° above the horizontal. Find the time of flight. Take g = 10 m/s².

Hint

Find the vertical component of the launch velocity.

Worked answer
  1. u_y = 20 × sin 30° = 10 m/s.
  2. T = 2u_y / g = 2 × 10 / 10 = 2 s.

Answer: 2 s

A ball is launched at 20 m/s at 30° above the horizontal. Find the maximum height it reaches. Take g = 10 m/s².

Hint

At the top the vertical velocity is zero.

Worked answer
  1. u_y = 20 × sin 30° = 10 m/s.
  2. H = u_y² / 2g = 100 / 20 = 5 m.

Answer: 5 m

Find the horizontal range of a projectile launched at 20 m/s at 45° above level ground. Take g = 10 m/s².

Hint

Use R = u² sin 2θ / g.

Worked answer
  1. sin 2θ = sin 90° = 1.
  2. R = 20² × 1 / 10 = 40 m.

Answer: 40 m
