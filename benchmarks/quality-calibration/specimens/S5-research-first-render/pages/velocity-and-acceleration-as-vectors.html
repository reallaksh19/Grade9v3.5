Velocity and acceleration as vectors

Key idea

Velocity has an x-part and a y-part. Speed is the size of the velocity vector, and acceleration is the change in the velocity vector per second.

Understand it

If x and y change with time, the velocity components are vₓ = Δx/Δt and v_y = Δy/Δt. The velocity vector joins them, and its size is the speed √(vₓ² + v_y²).

Acceleration is the rate of change of the whole velocity vector. A change of direction alone, with the same speed, is still a change in velocity and so needs an acceleration.

Always subtract velocity vectors component by component: Δv = v_final − v_initial.

Common slip: Subtracting the speeds (4 − 3 = 1) instead of subtracting the vectors gives a wrong change in velocity when the direction changes.

Worked example

A particle's velocity changes from (6 m/s, 0) to (0, 8 m/s) in 5 s. Find the average acceleration.

  1. Write the change in each component: Δvₓ = 0 − 6 = −6 m/s and Δv_y = 8 − 0 = 8 m/s.
  2. Find the size of the change in velocity: |Δv| = √(6² + 8²) = 10 m/s.
  3. Divide by the time taken: a = 10 / 5 = 2 m/s².

Result: 2 m/s², directed along (−6, 8).

Rebuild it yourself

A car goes round a bend at a steady 20 m/s. Explain whether it is accelerating.

Compare with a model answer

Yes. Its speed is constant but the direction of its velocity changes, so the velocity vector changes, and any change in velocity per second is an acceleration.

Practice

The position of a particle is x = 3t m and y = 4t m, with t in seconds. Find the speed of the particle.

Hint

Read off vₓ and v_y from how x and y change each second.

Worked answer
  1. x grows by 3 m every second, so vₓ = 3 m/s; y grows by 4 m every second, so v_y = 4 m/s.
  2. Speed = √(3² + 4²) = 5 m/s.

Answer: 5 m/s

A particle's velocity changes from 4 m/s along x to 3 m/s along y in 2 s. Find the magnitude of the average acceleration.

Hint

Subtract the velocity vectors, not the speeds.

Worked answer
  1. Δv = (0 − 4, 3 − 0) = (−4, 3) m/s, so |Δv| = 5 m/s.
  2. Average acceleration = 5 / 2 = 2.5 m/s².

Answer: 2.5 m/s²
