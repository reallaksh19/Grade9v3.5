Motion in 2D — Projectile Motion: Standard Model and Modified-Dynamics Demand

Start with the three-item diagnostic. Then use Core1 for orientation, Core1A/Core1B for teaching and reconstruction, Core2A for supported application, and Core2B for changed-demand transfer.

Diagnostic

At one instant, x-motion gives t=2 s. May the y equation use t=3 s for the same particle state?

Check

No. Independent components use separate axis equations but one shared physical time.

Routes to MIC-PHY-KIN-2D-INDEPENDENT-COMPONENTS

A projectile is at its apex. Which quantity is zero: total velocity, horizontal velocity, or vertical velocity?

Check

Only the vertical velocity component is zero in the standard no-drag projectile model.

Routes to MIC-PHY-KIN-PROJECTILE-MODEL

If a force proportional to velocity acts during flight, may the ordinary constant-acceleration projectile equations be used unchanged?

Check

No. First decide the modified force model; velocity-dependent drag makes acceleration non-constant.
