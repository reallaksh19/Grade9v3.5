Official paper locator

Governed faithful restatement

A projectile has coordinates x=24t and y=43.6t-4.9t^2, with x and y in metres and t in seconds. What angle does its velocity vector make with the horizontal at t=2 s?

Conditions

None supplied by the governed record.

Options

  • 60°
  • 45°
  • 30°
  • 75°

Subparts

No subparts in the governed restatement.

Figures and captions

None supplied by the governed record.

Source hints only

The source-custody record supplies no source hint.

Source-answer custody

45°.

  1. Differentiate the coordinate laws: v_x=dx/dt=24 m/s and v_y=dy/dt=43.6-9.8t.
  2. At t=2 s, v_y=43.6-19.6=24 m/s.
  3. Thus tan(theta)=v_y/v_x=1, so theta=45°.

Independent check: Equal positive horizontal and vertical velocity components at t=2 s require a 45° direction in the first quadrant.

Identity discriminator: Official 2018 Paper 2 Physics Q8 states a 45-degree projectile with H=120 m, loss of half the kinetic energy at the first ground bounce, rebound at 30 degrees, and asks the new maximum height; this matches the owner demand.

Governed faithful restatement

A ball is launched from level ground at 45 degrees and reaches a maximum height of 120 m. At its first impact with the ground it loses half its kinetic energy. Immediately after the bounce its velocity is at 30 degrees above the horizontal. Find the new maximum height.

Conditions

  • First flight starts and ends at ground level.
  • Half the kinetic energy is lost at the first bounce.
  • Post-bounce launch angle is 30 degrees.

Options

No answer options in the governed restatement.

Subparts

No subparts in the governed restatement.

Figures and captions

None supplied by the governed record.

Source hints only

The source-custody record supplies no source hint.

Source-answer custody

30 m.

  1. Initial maximum height gives u_y^2=2g(120). At 45 degrees, u^2=2u_y^2=480g.
  2. After losing half the kinetic energy, the speed squared becomes v^2=u^2/2=240g.
  3. The new vertical speed squared is v^2 sin^2 30 = 60g, so H'=60g/(2g)=30 m.

Independent check: The post-bounce height is one quarter of 120 m, consistent with half energy and a smaller vertical fraction.

Governed faithful restatement

A projectile has ordinary range d when launched at speed v and angle theta under gravity g. At the highest point it enters a region where the effective downward acceleration is g/0.81. If its new total horizontal range is d'=n d, find n.

Conditions

  • The acceleration changes only after the projectile reaches its apex.
  • Horizontal velocity is unchanged at the boundary.

Options

No answer options in the governed restatement.

Subparts

No subparts in the governed restatement.

Figures and captions

None supplied by the governed record.

Source hints only

The source-custody record supplies no source hint.

Source-answer custody

0.95.

  1. The projectile reaches the apex after covering half of its ordinary range, d/2.
  2. The apex height is unchanged, but the downward acceleration becomes g/0.81, so the fall time from the apex is 0.9 times the original descent time.
  3. The second horizontal segment is therefore 0.9(d/2); hence d'=d/2+0.9d/2=0.95d.

Independent check: Stronger effective gravity must shorten only the post-apex horizontal segment, so n must lie between 0.5 and 1.

Official paper locator

Governed faithful restatement

A projectile launched from O with speed v at angle theta passes through P, which is 5 m horizontally and 1 m vertically from O. Determine which source statements are correct about the required speed and whether the projectile reaches its maximum height before or after P for specified launch angles.

Conditions

  • Uniform gravitational acceleration g acts downward.
  • Point P has coordinates (5 m,1 m) relative to launch point O.

Options

  • (A) For theta=45°, v=5sqrt(g)/2 m/s
  • (B) For theta=45°, maximum height is reached before P
  • (C) For theta=30°, maximum height is reached after P
  • (D) For theta=tan^-1(1/5), v=125sqrt(g) m/s

Subparts

No subparts in the governed restatement.

Figures and captions

None supplied by the governed record.

Source hints only

The source-custody record supplies no source hint.

Source-answer custody

Options (A) and (B).

  1. Use y=x tan(theta)-g x^2/[2v^2 cos^2(theta)] at P=(5,1). For theta=45°, this gives v^2=25g/4, so v=5sqrt(g)/2; (A) is true.
  2. For the same 45° case, the trajectory slope at x=5 is negative, so the apex was passed before P; (B) is true.
  3. For theta=30° the apex is not after P, and theta=tan^-1(1/5) would make the straight-line term equal exactly to y=1 before gravity lowers the path, so no finite v can satisfy (D).

Independent check: The official final key marks AB.

Governed faithful restatement

A 0.2 kg projectile is launched at 270 m/s at 60 degrees in a medium with linear drag F=-c v, where c=0.1 kg/s. It hits a vertical wall after 2 s. Taking e=2.7 for the exponential evaluation specified by the source, determine the horizontal distance to the wall.

Conditions

  • Linear viscous drag acts opposite the instantaneous velocity.
  • Use the source's e=2.7 approximation.

Options

No answer options in the governed restatement.

Subparts

No subparts in the governed restatement.

Figures and captions

None supplied by the governed record.

Source hints only

The source-custody record supplies no source hint.

Source-answer custody

170 m (within the official accepted interval 167–171 m).

  1. The horizontal component obeys m dvx/dt=-c vx, so vx(t)=vx0 exp[-(c/m)t].
  2. Here vx0=270 cos60=135 m/s and m/c=2 s, so x(t)=vx0(m/c)[1-exp(-ct/m)].
  3. At t=2 s, x=270(1-1/e)=270(1-1/2.7)=170 m.

Independent check: The distance is smaller than the no-drag value 135×2=270 m, as it must be.

Official paper locator

Identity discriminator: Official 2023 Paper 1 Physics Q1 is a building/slide projectile with coefficient of restitution 1/sqrt(3), multiple statements and rebound height h1; it is a different demand from the owner label.

Governed faithful restatement

A ball slides without friction from a height h above a terrace whose top is 3h above the ground, leaves horizontally, strikes the ground and rebounds with coefficient of restitution 1/sqrt(3). Determine which of the source statements about launch speed, impact angle, rebound velocity and rebound geometry are correct.

Conditions

  • Slide is frictionless.
  • Ground is horizontal.
  • Coefficient of restitution is 1/sqrt(3).

Options

  • (A) u0=sqrt(2gh) horizontally
  • (B) rebound velocity equals sqrt(2gh)(x-hat - z-hat)
  • (C) impact angle is 60 degrees
  • (D) horizontal range divided by rebound maximum height is 2sqrt(3)

Subparts

No subparts in the governed restatement.

Figures and captions

None supplied by the governed record.

Source hints only

The source-custody record supplies no source hint.

Source-answer custody

Options (A), (C) and (D).

  1. Energy on the slide gives horizontal launch speed u0=sqrt(2gh).
  2. Falling through 3h gives vertical impact speed sqrt(6gh), so tan(theta)=sqrt(6gh)/sqrt(2gh)=sqrt(3), hence theta=60 degrees.
  3. After impact the upward vertical component is sqrt(2gh); rebound height is h, while pre-impact flight gives horizontal distance 2sqrt(3)h, so d/h1=2sqrt(3).

Independent check: The restitution factor maps sqrt(6gh) to sqrt(2gh), so the rebound vertical component is upward; this also shows option (B)'s sign/direction statement is not correct.

Primary capability preserved: CAP-KIN-PROJECTILE-MODEL
